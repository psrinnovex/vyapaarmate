## Full description draft

VyapaarMate helps customers interact with local businesses and helps approved business teams manage day-to-day work.

Customers can browse available businesses, review physical products or in-person services, place orders or appointment requests, and follow their status. Business owners and authorized staff can access the operational information made available to their role for their own business.

The business mobile experience is for existing approved accounts. VyapaarMate software subscriptions are not sold, started, upgraded, or renewed in the Android app, and the app does not direct users to an external subscription purchase.

Privacy and account controls include secure sign-in, session management, published mobile privacy information, and account deletion. Super-admin and support-agent workspaces remain available only through protected web and desktop surfaces.

Some functions require a network connection and services configured by the relevant business.
